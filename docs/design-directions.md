# Design directions

## Direction A — Working Proof

**Concept:** A precise, evidence-led editorial portfolio. Projects are “working proofs”: each shows the human problem, the system built, the evidence available, and the boundary of what can honestly be claimed.

- **Impression:** capable, curious, structured, early-career but serious.
- **Visual metaphor:** a continuous range line moving through `systems → data → interfaces → people`; it changes emphasis by project but never pretends to be measured data.
- **Typography:** confident sans for display/body; monospaced utility face for evidence, status, and technical labels.
- **Layout:** wide twelve-column desktop grid, narrow reading measures, strong left alignment, intentional asymmetry around project evidence.
- **Color:** pale mineral ground, dark pine ink, signal blue, warm safety orange, quiet blue-grey. Accents behave like annotations.
- **Navigation:** compact sticky header with Work, Approach, About, and Contact; case studies add local section navigation.
- **Projects:** problem and status first, architecture and decisions on dedicated pages.
- **Motion:** one orchestrated range-line reveal, local hover/focus response, no blocking intro.
- **Photography:** one prominent real portrait and selective project media. A clearly documented placeholder is used until the real portrait arrives.
- **Technical language:** diagrams, evidence tags, status, and source notes rather than dashboards.
- **Mobile:** range line becomes a vertical sequence; cards become stacked records; no horizontal dependency.
- **Accessibility:** content is meaningful without the line or motion; high contrast and explicit focus.
- **Performance risk:** low if the line is CSS/SVG and media stays selective.
- **Why Umar:** makes broad technical range legible while reinforcing honesty and architecture thinking.
- **Why not a template:** evidence boundaries and the range line arise from Umar’s actual profile and authorship constraints.

## Direction B — System Atlas

**Concept:** The homepage is an explorable map connecting C++, data, databases, web systems, AI-assisted practice, and real users.

- **Impression:** analytical, experimental, technically ambitious.
- **Visual metaphor:** an atlas of modules and connections.
- **Typography:** utilitarian grotesk with dense mono annotations.
- **Layout:** diagram first, panels second; projects open from nodes.
- **Color:** dark blueprint field with layer-specific signals.
- **Navigation:** map plus conventional fallback menu.
- **Projects:** each appears as a route through the system.
- **Motion:** connection tracing and state transitions.
- **Photography:** appears mainly in project detail and about.
- **Mobile:** map must collapse into a linear path.
- **Accessibility:** requires a complete text equivalent and careful focus management.
- **Performance risk:** medium to high; diagram state and animation can add JavaScript.
- **Why Umar:** expresses architecture and cross-layer thinking.
- **Why rejected:** risks becoming a decorative dashboard, makes recruiter scanning harder, and can overstate coherence across still-developing skills.

## Direction C — Field Practice

**Concept:** A warmer personal field journal of study, internship work, experiments, and technical notes.

- **Impression:** thoughtful, human, curious, approachable.
- **Visual metaphor:** observations from a developing practice.
- **Typography:** humanist sans with a restrained text serif for notes.
- **Layout:** photographic openings, short entries, marginal notes, and chronological clusters.
- **Color:** natural paper, graphite, field green, and photographic accents.
- **Navigation:** Work, Notes, Now, About.
- **Projects:** narrative essays with process images and reflective conclusions.
- **Motion:** page turns, gentle image reveals, no complex choreography.
- **Photography:** central; portrait and environment carry personality.
- **Technical language:** sketch-like diagrams and annotated excerpts.
- **Mobile:** naturally linear and readable.
- **Accessibility:** strong if annotations remain semantic.
- **Performance risk:** medium because high-quality photography is essential.
- **Why Umar:** leaves room for curiosity, philosophy, and growth.
- **Why rejected:** real photography is not yet available, the journal metaphor can underplay technical ownership, and it risks the familiar cream-and-serif portfolio default.
